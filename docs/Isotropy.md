# Isotropy quotients and the test comparison

Companion note to the paper *The join of two projective spaces: isotropy
quotients and the test comparison for De Morgan cubical sets*
(`docs/paperM/isotropy.tex`). Machine verification: `scripts/isotropy.py`.

## The problem

Two model structures live on De Morgan cubical sets with the same
cofibrations (all monomorphisms): the **cubical-type** structure of CCHM
cubical type theory (weak equivalences `W_type`) and Cisinski's **test**
structure (weak equivalences `W_test`, detected by the category of
elements), which presents spaces. The paper *Reversal is unrepairable*
showed the folklore witness against `W_type = W_test` is void and
re-opened the comparison problem. This paper proves the upper bound and
tests its sharpness on all isotropy quotients of the square.

## Results

1. **Upper bound.** `W_type ⊆ W_test` for every totally aspherical test
   cube category whose type structure is generated by pushout products of
   endpoint inclusions with monomorphisms. One-paragraph localizer
   argument. On cartesian cubes the inclusion is strict (Coquand's
   symmetric square); its contrapositive — test-nontrivial implies
   type-nontrivial — transfers element-category computations to the type
   side.

2. **Fixed-stratum retraction.** If a cyclic group's generator fixes a
   cell, the quotient is contractible in both structures: the
   equivariance constraints on a contracting homotopy solve around the
   cycle, leaving the interpolant free — an explicit contraction that
   connections make available. This uniformly contracts the symmetric
   square, the twisted square, and a Z/3 quotient of the cube (verified
   on all 256 literal points).

3. **Free quotients.** If the subgroup acts freely on cells, the
   quotient is a `K(H,1)` in the type structure — the syntactic fibrant
   replacement commutes with free quotients and the projection is a
   principal covering fibration — matching `el ≃ BH` on the nose.

4. **The join.** For the Klein group generated by the transposition and
   the double negation, no cell is fixed by the whole group, and the
   category of elements of the quotient is computed exactly:

       el(□²/K) ≃ B(Z/2) ⋆ B(Z/2) = RP^∞ ⋆ RP^∞

   — the join of two infinite projective spaces: simply connected,
   `H₂ = 0`, `H₃ = Z/2`. Proof: the strict quotient is the collage of
   its stabilized strata over the free stratum (no morphism flows from
   free to stabilized — machine-checked, zero violations in 23,040
   composites); the nerve is a double mapping cylinder whose pieces are
   `B(Z/2)`, `B(Z/2)`, `BK`, with interfaces `BK` mapping by the product
   projections — the definition of the join. The quotient is therefore
   contractible in **neither** structure. The full hyperoctahedral
   quotient is computed the same way (`π₁ = Z/2`).

5. **Syntactic census.** An independent, purely type-side proof of
   non-contractibility for the Klein quotient: the 72 twisted-symmetric
   squares form a graph under twisted-symmetric cubes with components
   {generic: 3, constants: 66, other: 3} — the generic square is
   isolated, and the isolation propagates through the syntactic fibrant
   replacement (the substitutions are automorphisms of the free De
   Morgan algebra, so no restriction clause fires). Positive and
   negative controls all behave as theory predicts.

6. **Non-separation.** Every tested quotient — the reversal circle, the
   symmetric/twisted squares, all free quotients, the Klein and
   hyperoctahedral quotients, the Z/3 cube — has the same answer in both
   structures. No automorphism quotient of the square separates the two
   localizers.

7. **Byproduct.** The De Morgan and Kleene cube categories are not
   Eilenberg–Zilber categories: the Kleene midpoint `x ∧ ¬x` has no
   (split epi, mono) factorization. Elegance-style techniques are
   structurally unavailable on these sites.

## The conjecture and the obstacle

We conjecture the agreement is general: the type structure assigns to
every isotropy quotient the homotopy colimit of its stabilizer strata —
the same diagram that computes the category of elements — and
`W_type = W_test` on De Morgan cubical sets.

The obstacle for the mixed cases is genuine: the geometric realization
of these quotients is contractible (a folded square is a triangle), so
cellular computations see nothing; and the stratification has no
presheaf-level counterpart, because every diagonal cell `(a,a)` is a
face of the free cell `(a, a ∨ (t ∧ ¬t))` — a Kleene-midpoint
phenomenon with no Boolean or cartesian analogue.
